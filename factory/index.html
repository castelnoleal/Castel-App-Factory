Update alerts

Let people know when a new version is out, even when the app is closed.

Tell people about updatesShows an update prompt in the app and a notification on the phone
Use Google Play's update promptFor copies installed from Google Play: Play downloads and installs the update in the app
Let pages show notificationsThe web Notification API works while the app is open (messages, reminders). Push while the app is closed needs a push service.
