Offline & caching

04
Generated apps expose CastelApp to HTML for persistent downloads and local files.
Offline storage is app-private persistent storage. Your HTML can download single files with CastelApp.downloadData(url,key,callback) or complete ZIP bundles with CastelApp.downloadBundle(url,key,sha256,callback). Use CastelApp.bundleUrl(key,path) to access bundled images, JSON, rulings, scripts, and other assets offline. Bundles are size-limited, path-traversal protected, SHA-256 verifiable, and activated atomically. Downloaded data survives app restarts and works without Internet.
