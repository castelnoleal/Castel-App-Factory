Privacy Policy

instApped — create and package web experiences as Android apps.

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how instApped (“instApped,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use our website, app-creation tools, and related build services. Please read it before using the service.

1. Information we handle

Depending on how you use instApped, information processed may include:

  • Project and app details: app name, Android package ID, version details, display settings, and project identifiers you enter.
  • Source content you provide: uploaded HTML or ZIP files, an app icon, or a website URL you ask us to package.
  • Build and download information: build identifiers, build status, and the APK/AAB artifacts produced for your project.
  • Local preferences and project data: the factory may save project details, settings, and source files in your browser’s local storage or IndexedDB on your device so you can return to your work.
  • Technical request information: our hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard connection, security, diagnostic, and service-usage data when requests are made.

2. How we use information

We use this information to operate instApped, save and manage projects on your device, validate build settings, submit build jobs, create and deliver APK/AAB files, troubleshoot errors, protect the service, and maintain reliability.

3. Build processing and service providers

When you request a build, the project details and source material needed for that build are sent to our build API. Build inputs and related metadata may be stored in a private source repository and processed by automated build workflows. Completed build files may be stored as private release assets and retrieved through the build service for download.

We rely on infrastructure providers, including Cloudflare for hosting and API delivery and GitHub for private source/build workflow storage and automation. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy and security terms.

4. Signing information

If you choose a signing feature, the service may validate a signing-access token to authorize the requested operation. Do not submit passwords, private keys, keystores, or other secrets unless a feature explicitly requires them and provides a secure method. Never place signing secrets in your website source code or public repositories. Signing credentials used by the service should be stored in restricted secret-management settings, not in public app content.

5. Storage and retention

Some project information remains in your browser until you delete it or clear the browser’s site data. Information sent for a build may remain in private repository branches, build logs, or release artifacts for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain build history, troubleshoot issues, or meet legitimate operational and security needs. Retention periods can vary by the service provider. We do not promise that clearing browser data deletes copies already submitted for a build.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your project content or personal information. We share or transmit information only as needed to operate the service, process builds, protect the service, comply with applicable law, or respond to valid legal requests. Information may be processed by our infrastructure providers as described above.

7. Public websites and third-party content

If you provide a website URL, instApped uses that URL to configure the generated Android app. The website itself may collect information from people who visit or use the resulting app. That collection is controlled by the website operator, not by this Privacy Policy. You are responsible for having permission to package the content and for providing any privacy disclosures required for your own app.

8. Security

We use private repository access and restricted configuration secrets for parts of the build pipeline. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please avoid submitting information that is not necessary for your project and keep your account credentials and signing material confidential.

9. Children’s privacy

instApped is a general-purpose app-creation service and is not designed specifically for children. Do not use the service to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Your choices and requests

You can remove locally saved project information by deleting projects within the factory where available or clearing the site’s browser storage. For questions or requests about information processed by the service, contact us using the address below and include enough detail for us to understand your request. We may need to retain certain build records for security or operational reasons.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service changes. The effective date at the top indicates when this version took effect. Continued use after an update means the updated policy is available for review; where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
